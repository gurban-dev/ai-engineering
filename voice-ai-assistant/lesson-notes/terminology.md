In machine learning, architecture is how the different components of a system are organised and interact with each other.

In the context of a voice AI assistant, architecture describes how audio enters your system, how it gets processed, and how a response gets returned to the user.

Architecture For this Project

Microphone: Capture's the user's voice (audio input).

        ↓

Speech recognition model: Converts audio into text.

        ↓

Language model: Interprets the text and generates a response.

        ↓

Text-to-speech model: Converts the response into audio.

        ↓

Speakers: Play the response to the user (audio output).
