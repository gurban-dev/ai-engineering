Try to answer each question without notes first, then explain your
reasoning in one or two sentences per item.

1. Define artificial intelligence in your own words.

   Artificial intelligence is technology that enables computers to
   perform tasks associated with human intelligence, such as
   understanding language, recognizing images, learning, and making
   decisions.

2. Give one everyday AI example other than Netflix or Google Maps,
   and describe its benefit.

   Spam detection is an everyday example of AI. It benefits users
   by identifying unwanted or dangerous emails and moving them out
   of the inbox. This helps reduce clutter and protects people from
   phishing attempts and other harmful messages.

3. Explain why a refrigerator can use fixed rules, but movie
   recommendations cannot rely on a complete rulebook.

   A refrigerator follows fixed rules because temperature control is
   predictable. Movie recommendations are harder because preferences
   are subjective, change over time, and depend on many interacting
   factors.

4. Describe the difference between training and operation in machine
   learning.

   During training, a machine-learning system studies many examples
   and learns patterns. During operation, it applies those patterns
   to a new case to produce a result.

5. A model sees thousands of labeled scans. What does it learn, and why
   is it not simply memorizing every scan?

   The model learns patterns in scans associated with cancer likelihood.
   It is not simply memorizing because its purpose is to recognize
   relevant patterns in new scans, not reproduce the exact training scans.

6. Explain why human experts remain important when AI makes predictions.

   Human experts remain important because AI can make errors, miss context,
   or reflect problems in its data. Experts set goals, check results, and
   make responsible decisions.

Key idea:
Machine learning learns patterns from examples rather than following a
complete rulebook.
