Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Retrieval means to find or extract information in the conext of
a computer.

Augmented means the information that was retrieved is added to
the prompt.

Generation

The AI generates an answer.

Why do is RAG needed?

An AI doesn't automatically know everything about your own documents.

The AI model will be asked:
What is John's favourite animal?

The AI model may not know anything about John.

The program will:
1. Search animals.txt

2. Find the revelant information:
   John's favourite animal is a penguin.

3. Give that information to the AI model.

4. Ask the AI model to answer using that information.

This is Retrieval-Augmented Generation.

USER -> "What is John's favourite animal?" ->

RETRIEVE Search the information ->

"John's favourite animal is a penguin." ->

AI GENERATES AN ANSWER ->

"John's favourite animal is a penguin."

The AI model is not searching the internet here. The program retrieves
information and gives it to the AI.
