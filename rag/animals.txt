Penguins are birds that live mostly in the Southern Hemisphere.
Penguins cannot fly, but they are excellent swimmers.
Emperor penguins are the largest species of penguin.
Penguins eat fish, squid, and krill.

Lions are large cats that live mainly in Africa.
Lions live together in groups called prides.
Male lions usually have large manes.
Lions eat animals such as zebras, wildebeest, and buffalo.

Elephants are the largest land animals on Earth.
African elephants have very large ears.
Elephants use their trunks to eat, drink, and communicate.
Elephants are herbivores and eat plants, leaves, bark, and fruit.

Dolphins are intelligent marine mammals.
Dolphins breathe air through a blowhole.
Dolphins use sounds to communicate with each other.
Some dolphins live in large groups called pods.
