Imagine a student collecting information from several school documents,
such as event notices or project instructions. Each document may organise
details differently, so the old way would be to open each document one
by one, find items like dates, names, locations, and costs and copy
everything into a table by hand. That takes time and makes copy-and-paste
mistakes more likely.

With AI, the same task could become a simple workflow:
• Upload suitable documents and explain the goal.
• Ask AI to extract specific details, such as dates, names, locations
  and costs.
• Have AI organise the details into a clear table.
• Check the table against the original documents for accuracy.

This can reduce repetitive copying and leave more time for understanding
the information. Use only documents for your school or family allows
you to upload, and never share private information without permission.

This is just one example of how AI can help. The objective is to use
AI to save time, increase productivity and protect privacy.

When you think about AI, think about tasks that normally require human
thinking, such as understanding speech, recognising a face, or choosing
a fast route.

Artificial intelligence is technology that allows computers to perform
tasks that normally require human intelligence.

AI does not automatically replace every developer or guarantee perfect
results; humans still define goals, check quality use it responsibly.

AI is already part of everyday life, even may seem new because of ChatGPT.

When it comes to Netflix recommendations, Netflix suggests movies and
shows you may enjoy, helping you avoid scrolling through thousands of choices.
These recommendations account for about 80% of Netflix viewing, demonstrating
how useful AI can be for finding content quickly.

Google Maps has arrival-time predictions. This estimates how long a trip
will take and updates that estimate as conditions will change. This helps
people plan when to leave and share a more realistic arrival time.

Billions of people rely on these predictions every day for commutes,
appointments and travel. AI is not only a recent chatbot trendl it has
been quietly helping people for years.

Before modern AI, engineers often created expert systems by programming
explicit, step-by-step rules for each situation.

A refrigerator is a example of this. A thermostat checks the temperature,
turns the cooling on when it is too warm, and turns it off once the target
temperature is reached.
